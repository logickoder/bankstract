# Voice

Applies to README, CHANGELOG, CONTRIBUTING, docstrings, comments, issue templates, commits, PRs, and error strings.

- Period-separated short hits. Several 3-7 word sentences beat one long one.
- **No em-dashes anywhere.** Use periods, commas, parens, colons. Split an aside into two sentences.
- Active voice. Concrete verb, concrete noun. No hedging (might, perhaps, we think, potentially).
- Concrete numbers, names and facts when available.
- No marketing words: seamless, powerful, robust, blazingly fast, intelligent, smart, AI-powered, agentic, supercharged, innovative, revolutionary, game-changer, effortless, world-class, industry-leading, leverage (verb), synergy.
- No apology theater: "We're sorry", "Oops", "Uh oh", "Don't worry", "Don't panic".
- No emojis in body copy.
- Lowercase bank names: palmpay, fbn, opay, zenith. `logickoder` always lowercase. Link github.com/logickoder in public copy.
- Errors follow `<what>. <why>. <do this>.` Three short sentences.
- Commits: subject 50 chars or less. Body only when the why isn't obvious.
- CHANGELOG: Added / Changed / Fixed. One line per change. Mark breaking ones.
